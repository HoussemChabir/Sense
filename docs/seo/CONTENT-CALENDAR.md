## Writing brief (applies to every post)

1. **First-hand.** Include at least three details only someone who has been there would know (a café name, a timing tip, a host quote). Use real photos taken for the post.
2. **Answer first.** Under each H2, give a direct 40–60 word answer, then the detail. This helps AI citations and featured snippets.
3. **One fact table** per post (prices, distances, opening times, seasons), each dated.
4. **Named author** with a bio, plus visible "Published" and "Updated" dates.
5. **One primary CTA** to the linked journey or `/tailor-made/`, as a WhatsApp link with prefilled text naming the post.
6. **No cannibalisation.** Each query family belongs to exactly one URL. Check this table before you write.
7. **Images** go through `npm run assets` so they stay visually lossless (SSIMULACRA2 ≥ 90) and get responsive widths.

## Refresh rules

- Refresh any post that drops more than 3 positions for its main query over 4 weeks.
- Re-date and re-check facts on safety, Ramadan, visa and price posts every 6 months.
- Merge posts that compete for the same query, and 301-redirect the weaker one.
